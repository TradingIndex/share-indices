How it works

What we post, what every number means, and what this is not.

Every weekday at 16:05 Beirut (9:05 New York), 30 minutes before the US open, we post the 5 liquid US stocks (price $10+, $50M+ traded a day) with the most unusual pre-market moves. Every Saturday the Movers Recap shows how they really closed.

The numbers on each card

Gap %Pre-market price at 9:00 New York vs yesterday's close.
Pre-market priceThe last pre-market trade before 9:00 New York. Not live.
x normal daily rangeThe gap divided by the stock's average daily move over 14 days (ATR). Above 1x = bigger than a whole normal day.
Volume yesterdayYesterday's volume vs its 20-day average: was attention already building?
Yesterday high / low / closeThe first levels day traders watch after the 9:30 open. Back to the close = the gap is "filled".
20-day high / lowAbout one month's extremes: a breakout, or a level price may stall at.

How the 5 are chosen

Score = (gap ÷ normal daily range) × √(yesterday's volume vs normal). We tested 4 ways to rank on 3 years of data (238,631 gaps): this one picked the stocks that moved most compared with their normal day (2.3x) and traded the most volume (about 4x normal), and it did so every year.

What this is not

Not buy or sell signals. A big gap tells you there was news and attention. It does not tell you which way the price goes next. Pre-market prices are thin and change fast after the open. Traders typically check the news, watch the first 5-15 minutes after 9:30, and watch price around yesterday's close, high and low before deciding anything.
Educational information only, not financial advice and not buy or sell signals. A big gap shows news and attention, not direction. Day trading is risky and most day traders lose money. Prices: Yahoo Finance pre-market data at 9:00 New York; they can be incomplete or wrong.
