Gap odds

What happened after gaps like this one: 238,631 gaps of liquid US stocks, 2023-12 to 2026-11. Enter the numbers from any card (ours or your own).

Don't know the x? Gap % ÷ normal daily range %. Example: gap 6% on a stock that normally moves 3% a day = 2x.

History, not a forecast. Daily bars, gap measured at the open (our daily list uses the 9:00 pre-market price). "Kept going" = closed beyond the opening price in the gap's direction. Not financial advice.
